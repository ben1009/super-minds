📚 Part 1 · 核心语法笔记 🎯

1. 介词 Prepositions — on, in, beside, at, under, between
① on — 面和面的接触
There is a book on the table.
There is a glass of water on the table.
② in — 在……里面
There is a cat in the room.
There are two cats in the room.
There are some gifts in my schoolbag.
③ beside — 在……旁边
Peter is beside Simon.
The table is beside the window.
There is a clock beside the window.
There is a chair beside the desk.
④ at — 在某处（固定搭配）
at bus stop — 在公交车站
at train station — 在火车站
at school — 在学校
⑤ under — 在……下面
There is a cat under the table.
⑥ between — 在……之间
Shannon is between Tiantian and Xander.
There is a hospital between school and home.
There is Lily between Jack and Leo.
介词 意思 例句
on在……上面（面接触）There is a book on the table.
in在……里面There is a cat in the room.
beside在……旁边There is a clock beside the window.
at在某处at school / at bus stop
under在……下面There is a cat under the table.
between在……之间Shannon is between Tiantian and Xander.
2. There is / There are + 介词短语

用法: 用 There is / There are 描述某处有某物，加上介词短语说明位置。

There is + 单数/不可数名词
There is a cat under the table.
There is a glass of water on the table.
There is a hospital between school and home.
There is a bag on the sofa.
There is a clock beside the window.
There is Lily between Jack and Leo.
There are + 复数名词
There are some children on the playground.
There are some gifts in my schoolbag.

💡 There is 后跟单数名词或不可数名词；There are 后跟可数名词复数。
💡 即使名词前有修饰语（如 Lily between Jack and Leo），也要根据名词的单复数选择 is/are。

3. 指示代词 this, that, these, those

用法: 用来指代特定的人或物。

词 意思 远近 单复数
this这个近处（就在身边）单数或不可数
that那个远处（离得远）单数或不可数
these这些近处复数名词
those那些远处复数名词
口诀: this/these 在近处，that/those 在远处
this/that 是单个，these/those 是多个

核心规则:
• this / that → 单数 或 不可数名词
• these / those → 可数名词复数
this / that — 单数或不可数
This car is mine. (单数可数名词)
That car is my dad's. (单数可数名词)
This water is clean. (不可数名词)
That bread on the table looks old. (不可数名词)
That food in the fridge is cold. (不可数名词)
these / those — 复数名词
These people are all my friends.
Those children are all my classmates.
更多例句
This bacteria is dangerous. (bacteria 作为"某种细菌"时视为单数/不可数概念)
That food in the fridge is cold. (food 通常作不可数名词)
4. 一般现在时的特殊疑问句 — 问什么答什么

疑问词: What (什么), Where (在哪里), When (什么时候), Which (哪个), Who (谁), Whose (谁的), How (怎样), Why (为什么)

非三单 (I / you / we / they)
What do you do on Sunday?
→ I play basketball on Sunday.
Where do you play basketball on Sunday?
→ I play basketball at school.
Where do they play basketball on Sunday?
→ They play basketball at school on Sunday.
三单 (he / she / it)
What does he do on Sunday?
→ He plays basketball on Sunday.
Where does Shannon play basketball on Sunday?
→ Shannon plays basketball at school on Sunday.
Where does he go swimming on Sunday?
→ He goes swimming in our town on Sunday.
疑问词 意思 例句
What什么What do you do on Sunday?
Where在哪里Where do you play basketball?
When什么时候When do the children go to bed?
Which哪个Which book do you like?
Who谁Who is your teacher?
Whose谁的Whose bag is this?
How怎样How do you go to school?
Why为什么Why do you like Science?

🍳 Reading 1: Keeping Food Safe in the Kitchen

Hello, children! Let me tell you about keeping food safe. This is my kitchen. It is clean and nice. Look at this tomato on the table. It is fresh. But that bread on the far shelf is old. Don't eat it!

孩子们，你们好！让我来告诉你们如何保持食品安全。这是我的厨房。它干净又漂亮。看桌子上的这个西红柿。它是新鲜的。但是远处架子上的那块面包是旧的。不要吃它！

These are my clean hands. I always wash these hands before I cook. Those are dirty knives over there, near the bin. Do not use those knives to cut food.

这是我干净的双手。我做饭前总是洗这些手。那边是脏刀子，在垃圾桶旁边。不要用那些刀子切食物。

This is raw meat. It has bacteria. That cooked chicken on the other plate is safe to eat. Keep this raw meat away from that cooked food.

这是生肉。它有细菌。另一个盘子上的那只熟鸡是安全的。把这块生肉远离那些熟食。

These vegetables are clean because I washed them. Those vegetables near the bin are dirty. Always wash these fruits and those vegetables before you eat them.

这些蔬菜是干净的，因为我洗过了。垃圾桶附近的那些蔬菜是脏的。吃之前一定要洗这些水果和那些蔬菜。

Remember: This rule is very important — clean hands, clean food, and a clean kitchen keep you healthy!

记住：这条规则非常重要——干净的手、干净的食物和干净的厨房让你保持健康！

Task 1: Multiple Choice (选择题)

1. ______ is my kitchen.

A. That
B. This
C. Those
Answer: B
(This is my kitchen. — 近处，用 this)

2. ______ bread on the far shelf is old.

A. This
B. These
C. That
Answer: C
(That bread on the far shelf is old. — 远处，用 that)

3. ______ are my clean hands.

A. This
B. These
C. That
Answer: B
(These are my clean hands. — 近处复数，用 these)

4. Do not use ______ dirty knives near the bin.

A. this
B. these
C. those
Answer: C
(Those are dirty knives over there. — 远处复数，用 those)

5. ______ cooked chicken on the other plate is safe to eat.

A. That
B. These
C. This
Answer: A
(That cooked chicken on the other plate is safe to eat. — 远处单数，用 that)
