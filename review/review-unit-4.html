📚 Part 1 · 核心语法笔记 🎯

1. would like — 四种常见句式

用法: 表示"想要"，语气比 want 更礼貌。
注意: would 没有人称变化，所有人称都用 would。

① 肯定句
结构: 主语 + would like + 名词（a/an/some...）
I would like an apple. / I would like some bread.
She would like a cup of tea.

💡 a/an 用在可数名词单数前；some 用在不可数名词或可数名词复数前。

② 否定句
结构: 主语 + would not (wouldn't) like + 名词
I wouldn't like a tomato.
She wouldn't like any bread.
③ 一般疑问句
结构: Would + 主语 + like + 名词？
Would she like a cup of tea? → Yes, please. / No, thank you.
Would you like an egg? → No, thank you.

💡 肯定回答：Yes, please.    否定回答：No, thank you.

④ 特殊疑问句
结构: What would + 主语 + like？
What would you like for breakfast? → I would like some bread for breakfast.
What would she like for dinner? → She would like some chicken for dinner.

💡 回答时一定用肯定句。

2. There Be 句式 — 某地有某物

用法: 表示"在某个地方有某种东西"，不强调它的所有人。
区别: Simon has a cat. (强调拥有) vs There is a cat on the floor. (强调存在)

① 肯定句
There is + 可数名词单数 / 不可数名词
There are + 可数名词复数
There is an apple in the basket.
There is some bread in the basket. (不可数名词当单数处理)
There are students in the room.

💡 There are 后面一定跟可数名词复数；其他的都用 There is。

② 否定句
There isn't + any + 可数名词单数 / 不可数名词
There aren't + any + 可数名词复数
There isn't any fruit in the fridge.
There aren't any grapes.

💡 否定句中用 any，不用 some。

③ 一般疑问句
Is there + any + 可数名词单数 / 不可数名词？
Are there + any + 可数名词复数？
Is there any bread in the basket? → Yes, there is. / No, there isn't any.
Are there any books in the library? → Yes, there are. / No, there aren't any.
④ 简短回答
肯定：Yes, there is. / Yes, there are.
否定：No, there isn't any. / No, there aren't any.
some / any & a/an 对比表（KET 必掌握）
情况 肯定句 否定句 / 疑问句
某物存在 some any
单数 / 不可数 There is + a/an / some Is there? / There isn't any
复数 There are + some Are there? / There aren't any

💡 可数名词单数用 a/an：an apple, a tomato, an egg, a pencil
💡 不可数名词用 some：some bread, some water, some chicken, some milk
💡 量词：a cup of coffee, two cups of coffee, a piece of bread

🛒 Reading 1: A Busy Morning at the Market

It is Sunday morning. Tom and his sister Amy go to the market with their mother. The market is very busy and noisy. There are many kinds of food. There are some fresh grapes, yellow lemons, sweet mangoes and big watermelons. There are also some brown potatoes, red tomatoes and a lot of brown eggs.

Tom is very hungry. He would like some bread with a fried egg. He also wants some fish because it looks delicious. Amy is not hungry, but she is very thirsty. She would like some cold juice. There are many lemons and mangoes, so she would like some lemon and mango juice.

Is there any bread at the market? Yes, there is. But there aren't any apples. Tom would like an apple, but he can't have any. He says, "I would like some sweet watermelons, please." Their mother is happy because everything is cheap today. She buys a lot of fruit and vegetables. They go home with heavy bags and big smiles.

Task 1: Multiple Choice (选择题)

1. Where do Tom and Amy go on Sunday morning?

A. To a restaurant
B. To the market with their mother
C. To school
Answer: B
(Tom and his sister Amy go to the market with their mother.)

2. Why does Tom want to eat fish?

A. Because it is cheap
B. Because it looks delicious
C. Because he is thirsty
Answer: B
(He also wants some fish because it looks delicious.)

3. What would Amy like to drink?

A. Cold water
B. Lemon and mango juice
C. Apple juice
Answer: B
(She would like some lemon and mango juice.)

4. Are there any apples at the market?

A. Yes, there are some fresh apples
B. No, there aren't any apples
C. Yes, but they are expensive
Answer: B
(But there aren't any apples.)

5. How do Tom and Amy feel at the end of the story?

A. Tired but happy
B. Hungry and sad
C. Thirsty and angry
Answer: A
(They go home with heavy bags and big smiles.)

🤖 Reading 2: AI at the Market

It is Sunday morning. Tom and his sister Amy go to a new market near their home. This market is different. It uses artificial intelligence (AI) to help people shop.

When Tom picks up a watermelon, a smart screen on the shelf shows a message: "This watermelon is very sweet and fresh today." Amy touches a lemon, and the screen says, "This lemon is sour. You can make delicious lemon juice with it."

Tom is hungry. He would like some bread and an egg. He also sees some red tomatoes. The AI suggests, "These tomatoes are perfect for a sandwich." Tom smiles and puts three tomatoes in his basket.

Amy is thirsty. She would like some cold juice. The AI looks at the fruit in her basket and says, "You have lemons and mangoes. Would you like to make some fresh juice?" Amy thinks it is a great idea.

Is there any bread? Yes, there is. But there aren't any apples today. The AI explains, "Apples will arrive tomorrow morning." Tom and Amy carry their bags home. They are not tired because the AI helps them find things quickly. Tom says, "I love this smart market. Shopping is so easy!"
