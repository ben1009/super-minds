💭 Part 2 · I don't think…

表达“我认为……不……”时，本课重点练习把 don't 放在 think 前。

本课目标表达 · 推荐练习

I don't think the shark is happy with us.

我认为鲨鱼对我们不太满意。

I don't think he likes me.

我认为他不喜欢我。

句子结构

I don't think + 肯定形式的句子

I don't think + the shark is happy…

I don't think + he likes me.

PDF 将 I think the shark isn't happy with us 和 I think he doesn't like me 列为本课不练习的表达。先掌握本课目标句型；这些表达在英语里侧重点不同。

用 PDF 的例句试一试

🐟 Part 3 · Fish, There were, and There are

fish 的单复数

fish 表示“鱼”时，常用的单数和复数形式一样。

one fishmany fish

PDF 例句：Many fish, one fish, a fish.

过去和现在

There were many sharks in the sea,

but now there are not many.

过去：There were…　现在：There are not…

What sort of…?

What sort of bird is that?

那是什么种类的鸟？

用来询问种类：What sort of + 名词…?

be good at · 擅长

be good at + 名词 / 动词 -ing

She is good at drawing.

He is good at English.

look for = 寻找

Let's look for the letter.

我们来找那个字母吧。

look at = 朝……看

Look at that giant shell.

看那只巨大的贝壳。

Fill in · for or at?

1. Let's look ______ the letter.

2. Look ______ that shell!

✍️ Part 5 · Four Uses of 's

看缩写所在的句子，判断它表示什么。

1 · 所有格 Possessive

my mom's bag · my dad's watch

我妈妈的包 · 我爸爸的手表

2 · is

She's my sister. · He's my brother.

She’s = She is；He’s = He is

3 · has

She's got two brothers. · He's got two sisters. · It's got two ears.

She’s got = She has got；He’s got = He has got；It’s got = It has got

4 · us in Let's

Let's go! · Let's play!

Let's = let us：让我们……吧。这个用法只在 Let's 这个表达里。
