📖 Story Background · 故事背景

Meet Ben and Lucy — they are the explorers. They find lost treasure and give it to museums.

来认识一下 Ben 和 Lucy —— 他们是探险家。他们找到丢失的宝藏并交给博物馆。

Their lives are very exciting. Today they are starting a new adventure in the castle.

他们的生活非常令人兴奋。今天他们要在城堡里开始一场新的冒险。

They are in a castle looking for an old book. The book tells the secret of some lost treasure.

他们在城堡里寻找一本古书。这本书讲述了一些丢失宝藏的秘密。

But they are not the only people looking for the book!

但他们并不是唯一在寻找这本书的人！

💪 Part 2 · be good at + doing 🎯

📋 动词原形 → -ing 形式

原形 -ing 形式 注意点
rideriding去 e 加 -ing
flyflying直接加 -ing
climbclimbing直接加 -ing
swimswimming双写 m 再加 -ing
dodoing直接加 -ing
playplaying直接加 -ing
skateskating去 e 加 -ing
runrunning双写 n 再加 -ing
💡 规则总结: ① 大多数动词直接加 -ing；② 以不发音 e 结尾 → 去 e 加 -ing；③ 重读闭音节（辅+元+辅）→ 双写末尾辅音再加 -ing
练习一 · 用所给动词的适当形式填空

句型: be good at + 动词-ing (擅长做某事)

1. I'm good at __________ (ride) bikes.

2. My sister is good at __________ (fly) kites.

3. Ben is not good at __________ (climb) trees.

4. Lucy is very good at __________ (swim).

5. We are good at __________ (do) puzzles.

6. The boys are good at __________ (play) football.

7. Are you good at __________ (skate)?

8. I'm not good at __________ (run) fast.

练习二 · 选词填空

选词: play basketball / fly a kite / ride a horse / do puzzles / climb mountains

1. My grandpa is good at __________. He can do 100 pieces in 5 minutes.

2. Tom is good at __________. He scores many points in the game.

3. Look! The girl is good at __________. Her horse runs very fast.

4. I'm not good at __________. I always fall down on the hills.

5. We are good at __________. The kite goes very high.

练习三 · 按要求改写句子（肯定句 ↔ 否定句）
例子: I'm good at swimming. → 否定句: I'm not good at swimming.
