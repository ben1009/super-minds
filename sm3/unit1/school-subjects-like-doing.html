❤️ Part 2 · like / love / hate / dislike + doing 📝

Like, love, hate, dislike + doing 表示习惯性动作，后面永远接 动词-ing 形式！

句型一 · like + doing（主语: I / We / They / You）

I like reading English stories.

我喜欢读英语故事。

We like learning geography.

我们喜欢学地理。

They like singing in music class.

他们喜欢在音乐课上唱歌。

You like studying history.

你喜欢学历史。

We like practicing dance.

我们喜欢练舞蹈。

句型二 · likes + doing（主语: He / She / 单个人名）

She likes reading English books.

她喜欢读英语书。

He likes studying geography.

他喜欢学地理。

Mary likes listening to music.

玛丽喜欢听音乐。

Tom likes learning about history.

汤姆喜欢学历史。

She likes running in PE.

她喜欢在体育课上跑步。

句型三 · don't like + doing（主语: I / We / They / You）

I don't like memorizing English words.

我不喜欢背英语单词。 memorize: 背，记

We don't like copying geography notes.

我们不喜欢抄地理笔记。 copy: 模仿，抄写 | notes: 笔记

They don't like practicing the piano.

他们不喜欢练钢琴。 practice: 练习，训练

I don't like running long distances in PE.

我不喜欢在体育课上长跑。 long distances: 长跑

句型四 · doesn't like + doing（主语: He / She / 单个人名）

She doesn't like speaking English in class.

她不喜欢在课堂上说英语。 speak: 说，讲

Tom doesn't like singing in front of others.

汤姆不喜欢在别人面前唱歌。 in front of: 在...前面 | others: 其他人

Mary doesn't like remembering history dates.

玛丽不喜欢记历史日期。 remember: 记住 | dates: 日期

My brother doesn't like doing push-ups.

我弟弟不喜欢做俯卧撑。

📋 规律总结

主语 用哪个 后面
I / You / We / They like / don't like + doing
He / She / 单个人名 likes / doesn't like
⚠️ 注意:
① likes 只用于肯定句（三单肯定）
② doesn't like 只用于否定句（三单否定）
③ 后面永远接 doing，没有例外！
⚠️ 易错点提醒
❌ 易错点 ✅ 正确做法
I likesI like（I 永远不用 likes）
She don't likeShe doesn't like
danceingdancing（去 e 加 ing）
runingrunning（双写 n 加 ing）
studingstudying（y 直接加 ing，不变 y）

1. My sister _______ (watch) TV after school.

A. like watch
B. likes watching
C. don't like watching
D. doesn't like watch

B. likes playing
C. don't like play
D. doesn't like playing

🔍 助动词专项选择题 · Do / Does (1–10) ✅

考察助动词 do/does 的选择 + like 后面动词的形式 — 点击选项查看答案

1. ______ your brother like ______ (play) basketball in PE?

A. Do / playing
B. Does / playing
C. Don't / play
D. Doesn't / play
答案: B

2. We ______ like ______ (dance) at school.

A. does / dancing
B. doesn't / dance
C. don't / dancing
D. do / dance
答案: C
We = 非三单，否定用 don't，like 后面用 dancing

3. ______ Mary like ______ (sing) in music class?

A. Do / singing
B. Does / sing
C. Do / sing
D. Does / singing

4. I ______ like ______ (read) history books.

A. don't / reading
B. doesn't / reading
C. don't / read
D. doesn't / read
答案: A
I = 非三单，否定用 don't，like 后面用 reading

5. ______ they like ______ (draw) cartoons in art class?

A. Does / drawing
B. Do / drawing
C. Do / draw
D. Does / draw
答案: B
they = 非三单 → Do，like 后面用 drawing

6. My sister ______ like ______ (study) geography. She thinks it's boring.

A. don't / studying
B. doesn't / studying
C. don't / study
D. doesn't / study
答案: B

7. ______ you like ______ (listen) to music after school?

A. Does / listening
B. Do / listening

D. Do / listen

8. His parents ______ like ______ (run) in PE class.

D. don't / run
