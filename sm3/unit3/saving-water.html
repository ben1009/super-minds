⚖️ Part 2 · Grammar for Saving Water

because · 因为

because 后面跟完整句子，说明原因。英语里 because 和 so 不能同时使用。

Water is dirty because it doesn't rain very often.

a lot of · 许多

a lot of 可以修饰可数名词复数，也可以修饰不可数名词。

There are a lot of students. He drank a lot of water.

There are + 名词 + who/that...

There are more people on the Earth who need water.

who need water 修饰前面的 people。

much vs many

much 修饰不可数名词：water, time, money。

many 修饰可数名词复数：people, students, books。

mean / means · 意味着

主语是 this 或 he/she/it 时使用 means。

This means that they use water when they don't have to.

are not using · 现在进行时否定

are not using = aren't using，强调此时或现阶段没有在做某事。

它和表示习惯的 don't use 不完全一样。

less, fewer, more

词语修饰什么例子
less不可数名词less water · less time
fewer可数名词复数fewer people · fewer students
more可数或不可数名词more people · more water
记忆口诀：less 修饰不可数，fewer 修饰可数，more 两者都可以。

📖 Part 4 · Let's Save Water!

阅读短文；点击每段显示中文翻译。

Water is important for life on the earth. Every year, there are more people who need water, but many places don't have much clean water. We have to save water, or we will waste it all.

水对地球上的生命很重要。每年，需要水的人越来越多，但许多地方没有多少干净的水。我们必须节约用水，否则会把水全部浪费掉。

How can we save water in our daily lives? First, turn the tap off while you brush your teeth. Have a shower, not a bath, because a bath uses more water. When you wash your bike, use a bucket of water instead of a hose-pipe.

我们怎样在日常生活中节约用水？首先，刷牙时关掉水龙头。洗淋浴，不要洗盆浴，因为盆浴用水更多。洗自行车时，用一桶水代替水管。

Even a dripping tap can waste a lot of water. You can do a test. Put a cup under a dripping tap and wait for ten minutes. Then measure the wasted water. You will see how much water is wasted every day. Let's start saving water now!

即使一个滴水的水龙头也会浪费很多水。你可以做一个测试。在滴水的水龙头下放一个杯子，等待十分钟。然后测量浪费掉的水。你会看到每天浪费了多少水。让我们现在就开始节约用水吧！

1. Why do we need to save water according to Paragraph 1?

A. Because water is dirty everywhere.
B. Because there are more people who need water, but clean water is limited.
C. Because there is too much water on the earth.
More people need water, but many places don't have much clean water.

2. What does the writer suggest when you take a bath?

A. You should have a bath instead of a shower.
B. You should use more water in the bath.
C. You should have a shower, not a bath.
A bath uses more water.

3. How should you wash your bike according to the passage?

A. Use a hose-pipe to wash it.
B. Use a bucket of water to wash it.
C. Use a cup of water to wash it.
Use a bucket of water instead of a hose-pipe.

4. In the dripping tap test, what should you do first?

A. Measure the water in the cup.
B. Put a cup under a dripping tap and wait for ten minutes.
C. Turn off the tap carefully.
First put a cup under a dripping tap and wait for ten minutes.
