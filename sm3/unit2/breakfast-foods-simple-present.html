📌 Part 2 · Useful Phrases 🧠

on Saturdays

In Britain, some families make a special breakfast on Saturdays.

在英国，一些家庭每周六做一顿特别的早餐。

lots of + 名词

There are lots of different foods on the table.

桌子上有许多不同的食物。

be full of food

The breakfast table is full of food.

早餐桌上摆满了食物。

live in...

Some people live in the north of Mexico.

一些人住在墨西哥北部。

a breakfast called...

They eat a breakfast called salchicha con huevo.

他们吃一种叫作 salchicha con huevo 的早餐。

try

Would you like to try some black tea with honey?

你想尝试一些加蜂蜜的红茶吗？

🍕 Part 3 · At the Pizza Place (order pizza) 💬

Waiter: Hello! Can I help you?

服务员：你好！需要帮忙吗？

Customer: Yes, please. I'd like a pizza with chicken and mushrooms.

顾客：是的。我想要一个加鸡肉和蘑菇的披萨。

Waiter: OK. Would you like any cheese on it?

服务员：好的。你想在上面加奶酪吗？

Customer: Yes, please. Have you got any onions?

顾客：好的。你们有洋葱吗？

Waiter: Sorry, we haven't got any onions today. How about some tomatoes?

服务员：抱歉，我们今天没有洋葱。西红柿怎么样？

Customer: OK. Can I have tomatoes and eggs too?

顾客：好的。我也可以加西红柿和鸡蛋吗？

Waiter: Sure! So that's a pizza with chicken, mushrooms, cheese, tomatoes and eggs.

服务员：当然！所以这是一个加鸡肉、蘑菇、奶酪、西红柿和鸡蛋的披萨。

Customer: Yes, please. How much is it?

顾客：是的。多少钱？

Waiter: It's $12. Enjoy your pizza!

服务员：12 美元。请享用你的披萨！

Customer: Thank you!

顾客：谢谢！

角色

Waiter（服务员）：Student A

Customer（顾客）：Student B

I’d like a pizza with...
我想要一个加……的披萨。
Have you got any...?
你们有……吗？
We haven’t got any...
我们没有……
Can I have...?
我可以加……吗？
How about...?
……怎么样？用于提出替代建议。
替换练习

I would like a yellow pen. / Can I have a yellow pen?

We haven't got any yellow ones. How about a red one?

🏠 Part 4 · A Busy Saturday at Home 📖

必做阅读。点击段落显示中文。

Today is Saturday. Everyone in the family is busy.

今天是星期六。家里的每个人都很忙。

Dad does the shopping at the supermarket. He buys some meat, vegetables and fruit.

爸爸在超市购物。他买了一些肉、蔬菜和水果。

Mum cooks in the kitchen. She makes a big pizza for lunch.

妈妈在厨房做饭。她午餐做了一个大披萨。

Ben is very helpful. He takes the dog for a walk in the morning. Then he washes up and dries the dishes.

Ben 非常乐于助人。他早上带狗散步。然后他洗碗并擦干盘子。

Lucy doesn't like cooking, but she does other housework. She sweeps the floor and tidies up the living room. She also feeds the dog.

Lucy 不喜欢做饭，但她做其他家务。她扫地并整理客厅。她还喂狗。

The dog's name is Buster. Buster is a big brown dog. He is very happy today because he gets a big bone!

狗的名字叫 Buster。Buster 是一只棕色的大狗。他今天很开心，因为他得到了一根大骨头！

After all the work, the family sit together and have a nice lunch. Ben says, "I think Lucy likes washing up now!" Lucy laughs and says, "No, I still don't like washing up, but I like pizza!"

所有工作完成后，全家坐在一起吃一顿美好的午餐。Ben 说：“我觉得 Lucy 现在喜欢洗碗了！”Lucy 笑着说：“不，我还是不喜欢洗碗，但我喜欢披萨！”

✅ Read and Choose

1. What does Dad do on Saturday?
A. He cooks lunch.
B. He does the shopping.
C. He sweeps the floor.
答案: B. He does the shopping.
2. Who takes the dog for a walk?
A. Mum
B. Lucy
C. Ben
答案: C. Ben.
3. What does Lucy do after she sweeps the floor?
A. She tidies up and feeds the dog.
B. She does the shopping.
C. She takes the dog for a walk.
答案: A. She tidies up and feeds the dog.

🔁 Part 5 · Simple Present Grammar ✍️

he / she / it + 动词 -s

Dad does the shopping.

Mum cooks in the kitchen.

Lucy feeds the dog.

复数主语 + 动词原形

Ben and Lucy tidy the living room.

They dry the dishes.

The children cook together.

否定句

Lucy doesn't like cooking.

Dogs cannot eat chocolate.

I don't like washing up.

拼写提醒: do → does, go → goes, wash → washes, tidy → tidies, dry → dries, have → has.

🧽 Part 6 · A Helping Hand at Home 📚

能力强的孩子阅读。点击问题卡片查看答案。

Every Sunday, the Johnson family has a big problem. Their house is always a mess because everyone is too busy during the week. Dad says, "We must tidy up the whole house today!"

每个星期天，Johnson 一家都有一个大问题。他们的房子总是很乱，因为大家工作日都太忙了。爸爸说：“我们今天必须整理整个房子！”

Ben usually takes the dog for a walk first. After he comes back, he starts to wash up all the dirty dishes from breakfast.

Ben 通常先带狗散步。他回来后，开始洗早餐留下的所有脏盘子。

Lucy usually hates doing chores, but today she says, "I'll help you, Ben. I can sweep the kitchen floor and feed the dog."

Lucy 通常讨厌做家务，但今天她说：“我来帮你，Ben。我可以扫厨房地板并喂狗。”

Lucy sometimes feeds the dog with chocolate cake, but dogs cannot eat chocolate! Ben always shouts, "Lucy! Don't feed Buster that!" Luckily, Buster never eats it. Lucy feels sorry and quickly gives him his dog food instead.

Lucy 有时用巧克力蛋糕喂狗，但狗不能吃巧克力！Ben 总是喊：“Lucy！不要喂 Buster 那个！”幸运的是，Buster 从来不吃。Lucy 很抱歉，赶快给他狗粮。

Meanwhile, Mum goes out to do the shopping. She buys vegetables, meat and bread. When she returns, she sees that Ben and Lucy tidy the living room and sweep all the floors. They also dry all the dishes and put them away.

与此同时，妈妈出去购物。她买了蔬菜、肉和面包。她回来时，看见 Ben 和 Lucy 整理客厅并扫了所有地板。他们还擦干所有盘子并把它们收好。

Dad feels very proud. He says, "You two are great helpers! Now let's cook a big dinner together." So everyone cooks in the kitchen. Ben chops the onions, Lucy washes the tomatoes, and Mum makes a delicious soup. Finally, the house is clean, the dog is happy, and the family enjoys a wonderful meal.

爸爸感到非常骄傲。他说：“你们两个是很棒的小帮手！现在我们一起做一顿丰盛的晚餐吧。”于是每个人都在厨房做饭。Ben 切洋葱，Lucy 洗西红柿，妈妈做美味的汤。最后，房子干净了，狗很开心，全家享用了一顿美好的饭。

From that day on, Lucy starts to like doing chores. She realises that helping at home is not boring; it is fun when you do it with your family!

从那天起，Lucy 开始喜欢做家务。她意识到在家帮忙并不无聊；和家人一起做的时候很有趣！
